# Auto follow-up agents and the global search

Read this before changing the Auto follow-up page and its agents' switches, or the global search and its Ask AI.

The menu's Agent team section (Agent 团队), after Integrations and before Settings, leads with Auto follow-up (自动跟进), the AI work the system starts on its own as four agents on one page, a tab each (`client/pages/crm-agents.tsx`, route and page grant `crm-agents` at `/crm/agents`, the tab its last segment, `/crm/agents/<agent>`, through the child `crm-agents-tab`, which renders nothing, and the first without one; the grant `crmPages` lists and existing sales sets received once by marker `crm-agents-v1`; the four were a menu entry each until they were gathered here, at the same addresses): lead follow-up, opportunity follow-up, approval review and task preparation. Task preparation (`server/providers/crm/task-preparation.ts`, `TaskPreparer`, skill `ai/skills/task-preparation`, on the follow-ups' jobs with payload entity `tasks`) prepares an open task that concerns a lead, a deal or a company for the people doing it, always as its owner whoever asks (its owner, a participant or the morning's pass), as a lead's or a deal's follow-up runs as theirs: the record, its tasks, comments, people and mail (no news of the company: nothing is looked up on the web, so the card's `company` is null, but on a revision, which keeps the old card's), returned as what the task should achieve, where things stand, the people, the questions, what to bring, the risks and the points to cover, an `ai` entry on the task's timeline read by its owner and participants alone (`doesTask` in `server/providers/crm/task-participants.ts`; `historyOf` hides it from everyone else, as an approval's review from all but its approver, since it reads their mail), drawn by `PrepPanel` in `client/components/crm/task-preparation.tsx` as a follow-up's card is, and asked for or rerun from the chip atop the task's page (`PrepStatus`, AI 准备; `POST /api/tasks/:taskId/prepare`, `GET …/preparations/latest`, `POST …/preparations/:entryId/decide`); it waits in the task owner's inbox suggestions (`pendingDecisions` lists `tasks` entries for their owner while the task is open and its time ahead; its participants read it on the task), the task list's rows show its state as a lead's do, and it changes nothing, its accept (by one of them who may edit the task) writing the points to cover and what to bring under To prepare and the questions under To ask in the task's description (`mergePlanDescription`, the owner's own text kept). Its one trigger is the daily pass (`scanTaskPreparation`, scheduler task `crm.task-preparation`, target `app.crm-task-preparation`, 09:00 in the CRM's time zone): every open task with a time of day still ahead today, a record and an owner, once, at most 20 a pass; a running AI reads alike everywhere: `AiStateTag` (`client/components/crm/ai-state-tag.tsx`, words in `ai-state-words.ts`) on a list's row and a board's card, and the chips atop a lead's, a deal's, an approval's and a task's page in its tint and words while the AI is at work (a waiting suggestion keeps the chip's own name and word), the chip in every state and the first-run buttons (AI follow-up, AI 辅助审批, AI 准备) on the same tint (`bg-primary/10`, as the `ai` badge) without a border, the chip's word telling where it stands. On a timeline, a follow-up's, a review's and a preparation's line says where it stands (waiting, accepted, approved or rejected, ignored) as a tag (`EvaluationFeedEntry`, `ReviewFeedEntry`, `PrepFeedEntry`), a review's recommendation left to its card. Each page says what the agent does and its skill (a link to it on the Agents plugin's Skills page, `/skills/<id>`, for whoever may open that page, by the library ids the workspace reports as `methodSkills`), and that the AI runs it and only suggests (`agents.byAi`, the task agent's `agents.byAiTasks`), the owner deciding in the inbox, carries an On or Off tag beside its title, lists the triggers it runs on with a switch each and a switch for the agent as a whole (`server/providers/crm/agents.ts`: `AGENT_TRIGGERS`, stored in the `crmSettings` row `agents`, every one on unless an administrator switched it off through `PUT /api/aiAgents/:agent/switches`, which needs the settings capability), how its runs fared over the last 7 or 30 days (runs, accepted, dismissed, waiting, failed, the share accepted of those decided, its card without a line under it) and its runs of the last 90 days, newest first, filtered by status (`GET /api/aiAgents/:agent`, `agentOverview`, the `ai` entries of the records the reader may read, an approval's review only for its approver). Every automatic start passes `agentOn` before it runs: a lead created (now started with trigger `created`), an opportunity opened (`opened`), an approval submitted, the daily lead and deal checks (their signals fall under `daily`), a task done, a customer's reply, an approval decided, a lead's company changed and leads merged (`followUpLead` and `followUpOpportunity` check it for every trigger they are given); a person asking for a follow-up is never switched off. The metadata carries the switches as `agents`, and every "AI follows up at once" option (`FollowUpOption`, which takes its agent and trigger, and the outcome dialog's) shows only while that would happen (`agentRuns` in `client/components/crm/api.ts`). The menu shows the four agents' runs under way beside Auto follow-up as a live badge, a pulsing dot in the primary colour and the count, "N running" on hover, and each tab its own as a pulsing dot and count (a tab of an agent switched off says Off instead) (`GET /api/aiAgents/running`, `useRunningAgents` in `waiting-count.ts`, read every few seconds while any runs; `NavigationBadge` in `navigation-badges.ts` takes a number or a live badge). The Switch primitive is the registry's `client/components/ui/switch.tsx`.

The global search, ported from NocoITAM's: a command palette (`GlobalSearchDialog` in `client/components/crm/search-dialog.tsx`, the shadcn `command` primitive over `cmdk`, a devDependency) that ⌘K / Ctrl+K opens anywhere in the application layout (never over another dialog, and only where the dialog is mounted), or the search button at the right of the sidebar's brand (`SearchHeaderButton`, its hover ground a 7-unit box pulled back by its margin so the icon keeps its place, through the `action` slot `AppSidebar` gained for it, an application change to the template's sidebar; in the phone's sheet beside its close button), which in the collapsed sidebar, having no room there, is the menu's Search under the overview instead (`SearchNavButton`, placed through `NavigationMenu`'s `after` option keyed by route name, an application change to the template's menu, since a route's `navigation` cannot open a dialog). Its state lives above the dialog in `GlobalSearchProvider` (`client/components/crm/global-search.tsx`, context in `global-search-state.ts`), a React provider of its own (`crm-global-search` in `client/react-providers.ts`, inside `crm-agent-chat`), so what was typed outlives a move to settings. Keyword search is `GET /api/search?q=` (`searchAll` in `server/providers/crm/search.ts`): each kind's own list with its search, five rows and a total per kind, a kind the reader may not read left out rather than refused; the list search itself (`CrmService.filtered`, so the list pages and the agents' list commands too) matches a phone-like text whole and anything else word by word in any order across name, company, email, phone and website, a deal and a contact also through their company's name (`companiesNamed`: the companies the reader may read whose name holds the word, since they link their company rather than hold its name), splitting on the LIKE wildcards rather than escaping them, since the query layer has no ESCAPE. A lead's, a contact's and a deal's result reads as its page heads it (`recordTitle`: "name / company", "company / name"), the company then left out of its particulars. A result opens the record's page, "All N" its list with `?search=`. A list page's own keyword is a filter of that list, apart from the global search: its toolbar button is a filter icon ("Filter this list", `/` focusing its box, ⌘K staying the global search), the box reads "Filter leads…" (`filterIn`) among the other conditions, and a set keyword shows above the list as a chip that clears it, beside how many records the conditions leave (`keywordChip`, `matchCount`; `client/pages/crm-list.tsx`). Its last row, "Ask AI: …" (`globalSearch.askAbout`), kept in view at the list's foot over a rule however many results scroll above it, answers what was typed in the dialog itself (`SearchAnswer` in `client/components/crm/agent-chat/search-answer.tsx`, `useSearchAnswer` in `ask.ts`): a conversation of the source `crm-search` is created and the question sent, the reply drawn as it is written, and no conversation list shows it (`listedConversations` in `client/components/crm/agent-chat/ask.ts`, used by the panel's history, the conversations page and the overview's latest); Continue asking keeps it (`POST /api/searchAnswers/:conversationId/keep`, which changes its source to `crm`, an ordinary conversation of the CRM's, the lists then refreshed) and opens it in the panel, while going back to the results or closing the dialog stops a reply still being written and deletes the conversation (`DELETE /api/searchAnswers/:conversationId`), and the daily scheduler task `crm.search-answer-sweep` (target `app.crm-search-answer-sweep`, 03:00 in the CRM's time zone) deletes those left an hour or more by a window closed on them, and turns the ones kept earlier in the `crmSettings` row `search-answers` into the CRM's conversations once (`server/providers/crm-agents/search-answers.ts`, `@temporary(nocobase-official)`, the Agents plugin having no way to delete a conversation or change its source); it is disabled with the reason while the assistant cannot answer (`useAgentReadiness`) and absent while the metadata's `assistant` is off. The search has no answer of its own any more: the former ask mode, its answer card, the page tool `presentRecords` and the hidden search conversation went with the AI Employee plugin. The list pages still narrow to given records through `?ids=`, under a chip named by `?found=`, which nothing in the search links to now.
