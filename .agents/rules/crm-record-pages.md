# Record pages, timeline and comments

Read this before changing a record's page or drawer layout, its particulars, the timeline, comments and replies, the change history, or long text and location fields.

`client/components/crm/lead-evaluation.tsx` sums up the latest verdict in a chip (`EvaluationStatus`, polling while it runs; asking for an evaluation is `EvaluateButton` beside the heading until the first suggestion and, after it, Follow up again at the end of the suggestion's chip, both on `useFollowUpRequest` in `client/components/crm/follow-up-request.ts`; no evaluation card offers it); every record page (lead, opportunity, task, company, contact, approval) shares one layout, `DetailColumns` with `DetailTabBar`, `InfoGroup` and `InfoRow` in `client/pages/crm-detail.tsx` (no `PageHeader`: the record's heading over the left column, the particulars beside it reaching to the top of the page, with its actions on a row under it, from the left, in a drawer and on a phone alike (left out when there are none), and the More menu (deleting) at the end of the heading's own line (`more` of `DetailColumns`) (stacked, the heading comes first, then what the record says of itself, a lead's original message, then the particulars; a drawer keeps its sides as narrow as its bar's, its stacked column keeping 8px each side, given back by its block's margin, so an inline editor reaching past the text is not cut by the column's scrolling) — the main one first (convert a lead, record a task's outcome; a lead's Convert in black, the button primitive's `contrast` variant, an application change, so it stands apart from the AI's purple chip beside it), `EvaluateButton` before the AI's first suggestion, then More with delete — and, on a lead's, a deal's and an approval's page, the AI's latest suggestion chip leading those actions under the heading (`status` of `DetailColumns`, nothing before the first suggestion: the AI's mark, the words AI follow-up and where it stands in a word (`AiStatusWord` in `client/components/crm/ai-status-word.tsx`, words `aiChip.status.*`: waiting, accepted, ignored, replaced, failed), the chip in the primary colour alone (its name, the word lighter, the rule before Re-run faint, only a failure in the destructive colour), in the buttons' type size, its hover saying the verdict, the reason, where it stands and that a click opens it in the timeline, then Re-run (重新运行), its full name on hover (an approval's review chip and a task's preparation chip alike)); below it the tabs atop the left column, which takes the room (under the heading above them, `intro` of `DetailColumns`: a lead's original message, a deal's description, a company's introduction, an approval's or a task's description), and the grouped particulars in a narrow column on the right, the tabs wrapping when they do not fit, each column scrolling on its own, the left one only below its tabs: the tabs stay, the intro folds away once the tab's content is scrolled down and returns at its top (`introAway` in `DetailColumns`), a long heading then keeping one line cut with an ellipsis, a long intro unfolded scrolling within at most 40% of the screen, and the tab's content scrolls with its scrollbar beside it alone (its room kept, so switching tabs re-wraps nothing; every scrollbar in the application, across and down, is a 4px rounded bar in a faint grey on no track, drawn only while its element scrolls and transparent otherwise (`client/styles.css`, through the `-webkit-` pseudo-elements, the standard properties only where those are missing, since Chromium ignores the pseudo-elements wherever the inherited `scrollbar-color` is set; `revealScrollbarsWhileScrolling` in `client/components/crm/scrollbar.ts`, installed once from `client/index.tsx`, an application change to the entry, marks whatever scrolls `data-scrolling` through one capturing listener on the document until it has been still for 0.8 seconds)), the timeline's comment box sticking atop it (stacked on a narrow screen, the particulars first, the page scrolling and the tabs and the comment box sticking to its top the same way, the box right under the tab bar, whose height `DetailColumns` measures as `--crm-tabs-h`, so no entry shows between them), the heading and tabs drawn once; a lead's person has a gender as a contact does (`202610100004_crm_lead_gender`, optional, carried to the contact a conversion creates), and a company an annual revenue in a currency, as a lead does for its company (`202610100007_crm_lead_revenue`, carried to the company a conversion creates), kept as a deal's amount is and, as it, read as one value and edited with its currency in a pop-up beside the column, as a location is (`MoneyField` in `client/components/crm/money-field.tsx`; `annualRevenue` and `currency`, `202610100005_crm_company_revenue`; the demo dataset fills both, and the leads' genders); an opportunity's stage is a `StageField` row whose every save also clears the lost and pause reasons and the resume date, with the stage's win probability read only beside it (and beside each open stage in its menu) rather than a row of its own, there being no stage stepper any more; companies, contacts and approvals use the same layout, an approval's description under its heading, and there is no other record layout any more), while the verdict itself is handled in the lead's timeline: `HistoryFeed` draws a waiting AI entry open, to be handled, until the reader closes it, and every other AI entry closed, its line saying where it stands, opened from that line (or on arrival, for the entry the page was sent to), the card then leading with what started it rather than repeating the line's title and verdict (`inFeed`), what started it a line of small grey text, its conclusion a sentence of its own above the list (as an approval review's is), and under it, always shown, the few sentences the AI writes of what it found (`brief`, a field both follow-ups' verdicts gained; each part with its own action where it is drawn (the email box its composer and Send email, which sends it alone and accepts the card as that, the field box its Write, still offered on a card so accepted), and at the card's foot, under a rule, the box that redoes it in the person's words, then Discuss at the left and, at the right, Ignore and Accept all, which does the whole of it (the ticked fields written, the email sent through the box as it stands, the task, the status) and lists on hover what that is: a waiting evaluation as the actionable `VerdictPanel`, any other as its read-only record, and scrolls to an entry named by `?focus=` or by the banner, lighting it up, bringing it back as the entries above it settle and leaving room under the timeline while it is focused so it reaches the top however few follow it (`FocusRoom`, measured against the column that scrolls to just what the entry needs, none once the entries after it fill the view, so the inbox's pane, always sent to a card, cannot scroll on into blank space) (accepting plans the first contact, nurtures or invalidates through `CrmService` as the person deciding), the detail pages' history tab is now the timeline `HistoryFeed` in `client/pages/crm-detail.tsx`, with the record's comments in it, there being no Comments tab: a box atop the timeline adds a comment (`CommentComposer`: the writer's initials beside it, one line high until more is written; @ and a name mentions a colleague through `MentionTextarea` in `client/components/crm/mention-textarea.tsx`, the active colleagues with a CRM role listed under the box, and the comment keeps whom its text still names as `changes.mentions`, `mentionsIn` in `client/components/crm/mentions.tsx`, which also draws each "@Name" as a mention, `MENTION_CLASS` (the theme's info colour, on no fill; in a long comment a `#mention` link the CRM's `Markdown` draws so), and under the box, which is transparent but for its caret, a layer copying its box model draws its text, each picked name in the mention's colour without its weight, since a textarea holds plain text; `CrmService.addComment` and `editComment` keep only active accounts other than the writer and, once saved, start one `record-notification` run with event `mentioned` for each colleague the comment newly names, an edit telling only whom it adds; whoever may not read the record is never told, since delivery reads the record as its recipient), and each comment, with its replies, is a card of its own (white, a faint border and shadow) set apart from the activity around them; a comment reads as Clarify shows one: on its entry's line right after its author's initials (`CommentAvatar`, `size-6`, the thread's lines placed from its size in `ThreadLine` and its text indented `pl-8` to line up after it) and name, under a speech-bubble icon (`MessageSquareText`), when it fits, otherwise whole below that line (`CommentExcerpt` measures, keeping the one-line text unseen in place), its author's edit and delete appearing on the line on hover (`CommentActions`, editing below with `CommentEditor`); an entry's actor is in bold and its time reads how long ago, compactly (`relativeTime` in `client/components/crm/api.ts`: "3m ago", "1d ago" in English, "3分钟前", "昨天" in Chinese; the full time on hover), and in Chinese a field's name follows "更新" with no space; an approval's decision comment and a task's result are kept on the status entry that closed it (`changes.decisionComment`, `changes.result`) and read under it as a long comment is, an older entry taking the record's own for its latest closing and a task created already done showing its result on its creation, so neither page has a row for them; a record's Tasks tab lists the open ones soonest first, untimed last and newest first among those (`orderBy=dueAt`), and under them, headed Closed, the completed and cancelled ones, the latest closed first (`TaskCards`), and its New task opens with the record named on its chip (the form gets the record's name as `titles`); comments are `crmHistory` rows with action `comment` and the text in `changes`; a comment may be answered: anyone who reads the record replies from the comment's line (Reply, `ReplyBox`), a reply being a comment whose `replyToId` (`202610100002_crm_history_reply_to`) names the comment or reply it answers, read under its comment at one level, oldest first, so the exchange reads in the order it took place (a reply to a reply too, as Slack, Linear and GitHub thread them), each with its author's initials, joined to the comment's by one line through their middles in the timeline's colour, clear of each by 6px as the timeline's line is of its icons (`ThreadLine`), and saying whom it answers (`comments.replyTo`), which, clicked, brings that message into view and lights it up for a few seconds in a faint tint, the AI's for the AI's own work (a follow-up's card) and the info blue for anything else (`flash` in `client/components/crm/flash.ts` with its `tone`, and `.crm-flash` / `.crm-flash-ai` in `client/styles.css`, as every jump to a place lights what it lands on: a timeline entry `?focus=` names (an AI entry on its card alone, `data-ai-card`, not its line), a comment's or a reply's whole message (its line, text and files, one `data-comment` block, lit across its card to the card's edges), and a mail conversation; in the inbox, picking the card already shown lights its entry anew, `refocus` in `flash.ts` sent from `client/pages/inbox.tsx`; unfolding the replies when it is among the folded), the reply box at the thread's end, drawn as the comment box a size down (the writer's initials, joined to the thread's line, the paperclip at its end, the shortcut, Cancel and Reply below), opening empty (anew for each it answers; whom it answers is told as a writer in the thread), a thread of more than three showing its first reply and its last with the ones between folded behind "Show N more replies" (`CommentThread`; `historyOf` leaves replies out of the paged list and gives each comment every reply under it, and the follow-ups read them with their comment and whom each answers), telling everyone who has written in the thread (event `replied`, but not whom it mentions, who are told so already), and a comment or reply deleted while it has replies keeps its place, reading as deleted (`changes.deleted`), until the last reply under it goes; a comment or a reply carries files (`changes.attachments`, file ids, its text then optional), picked with the box's paperclip, dropped onto the box (which lights up while files are held over it) or pasted into it, and uploaded at once through `POST /api/attachments/upload` with `recordId`, which asks only to read the record (`useCommentUploads` in `client/components/crm/comment-uploads.ts`), shown under the text one a row (`CommentFiles`: the kind's icon on a tile in a status colour, or an image's own picture; the name and size; at the row's end the date, where it came from, download and, while written, remove), each opening the preview dialog; the record's Attachments tab lists its files the same way (`FileCards`), a group with none left out (`CommentFiles` in `comment-files.tsx`), changed with the comment in its editor; a contact's tab shows once its comments carry files), and the follow-ups read their names with the comment (`files` in `commentsOf`); added by anyone who may read the record through `POST /api/:entity/:id/comments`, changed or removed only by their author through `PATCH` / `DELETE /api/comments/:id`, the text before an edit kept in `previous` and its time in `editedAt` from `202609300002_crm_history_note_edits`, an edit also leaving a `commentEdited` entry, which the timeline leaves out since the comment says it was edited, and a removal a `commentDeleted` entry without the text; `?action=comment` on the history endpoint reads the comments alone; `CrmService.addComment`, `editComment`, `removeComment`; the assistant proposes one as a plan's comment step (`comment.create`), and a lead's evaluation reads its comments and its tasks, the outcome of a completed one included), and the inbox's **AI suggestions** (AI 建议; once the Waiting on me page, 待我处理, whose address `/crm/decisions` now redirects there, an `entry` becoming `decision`): the inbox (`/inbox`, the template's route given navigation and placed right under the overview, above Tasks and Approvals, the menu's sections after them) renders its UI Library block with a registry from `useCrmInboxRegistry` (`client/components/crm/inbox-decisions.tsx`; the list and detail in `inbox-decisions-view.tsx`, the query and wording in `inbox-decisions-data.ts`): a collection category `crm-decisions` offered in the To do view alone (in All its group would follow every loaded notification), then the notifications, the template's decision category left out since the CRM sends no notice of kind decision; it is shown while the assistant is on to whoever holds the page grant `crm-decisions` (given to existing sales sets once by marker `crm-decisions-v1`, and still required by the redirect route); it lists those waiting, and the handled ones after them, the reader's own alone (a colleague's is handled from the record's page; there is no mine/team switch), the handled ones always listed after those waiting, and no heading of its own; a wide screen opens the first one beside the list.

The change history (`crmHistory`, written by `CrmService.history`) records for an update only the fields whose value changed, their new values in `changes` and their earlier values in `previous` (`202609230001_crm_history_previous_values`), which `HistoryChanges` in `client/pages/crm-detail.tsx` shows as → new with each object's own field definitions, the old value on hover when there was one, one person's updates one after another, of the same fields or within half an hour of each other, reading as one save of every field they changed, each from the value before the first edit to the last (`collapseEdits`; status moves only when they move the same field), each value on one line, cut where the line ends (the whole of it on hover, and a long text's or a street address's in a pop-up on a click, `LongTextPeek` in `client/components/crm/long-text-field.tsx`); a save that changes nothing records no entry. A lead's, company's, opportunity's or contact's history also carries the entries of the tasks linked to it that the reader may read (`CrmService.historyOf`: created, a status set to completed or cancelled (`TOLD_STATUSES`), and edits of the fields in `TOLD` (due time, owner) with only those fields, each with the task as it now reads under `task`; such a record's history is filtered and paged in code, since which fields an edit changed is in its JSON), drawn by `TaskEvent` in `client/pages/crm-detail.tsx` under the Tasks tab's icon, a completed one's result on its line when it fits and otherwise whole below it, as a comment's text; an opportunity's history likewise carries its readable approvals' status changes (submitted, approved, rejected, withdrawn, never their AI reviews), drawn by `ApprovalEvent` under the Approvals tab's icon with the decision comment, and a change of a deal's contacts reads as each contact with their role under the Contacts tab's icon; a task's other edits stay in its own history, and deleting a task writes an `taskDeleted` entry (its name, type and start) on each record it was linked to.

Long texts (`LongText` in `client/components/crm/common.tsx`: an introduction, the original message, a description, a comment) are Markdown, rendered by the CRM's `Markdown` (compact, line breaks kept, HTML escaped, images only from this origin) under the four-line fold, and read as one plain line where a line is all there is (`markdownLine` in `client/components/crm/line-breaks.ts`). In a record's particulars, the narrow right column, a long text instead previews three lines that fade out while there is more and opens, on a click, into a pop-up beside the column, about twice its width, to read it whole and edit it there with a double-click on the text, saved as an inline field saves: when the pop-up closes, Escape leaving it as it was, Cmd/Ctrl+Enter at once, (`LongTextField` in `client/components/crm/long-text-field.tsx`, through `InlineField`'s `longText='popover'` in the record pages' `inline` helpers; an empty one opens straight into the editor), so the column never jumps and editing has room. A lead's original message, across the left column under its heading, shows five lines that fade out and unfolds in place on a click, folding back on another (`FoldingText`, `InlineField`'s `longText='fold'`; a click waits a moment so a double-click, which edits, toggles nothing); the timeline's long texts (a comment, a task's result, a decision's comment) fold the same way. A website reads as a chip of its domain on a grey fill, in the value's own weight, its whole address on hover and in what the row's copy button takes (`data-copy`), its edit pencil beside the copy button and drawn as it is (`ROW_ACTION`: the icon muted on the card's white, with no border, a faint fill under the pointer). A lead's or a company's region and street address are one Location row (地址; `LocationField` in `client/components/crm/location-field.tsx`), its region on the first line as the reader's language writes it ("Hangzhou, Zhejiang, China"; 中国 浙江省 杭州) and the street address, which may run to several lines, muted under it in three fading lines as the column's other long texts (`FadePreview`), and edited as Clarify edits a primary location: a click opens a pop-up beside the column, as a long text's does, whose search finds a province or a country by its English or Chinese name (`GET /api/geography/places`, `searchPlaces` in `server/providers/crm/geography.ts`, there being no list of cities) and fills both, over the country, province, city and address each to change by itself, all saved together when the pop-up closes, as an inline field saves (Escape discards; Enter in the city or Cmd/Ctrl+Enter saves at once).

The comment box and the reply box are worded and sent as the UI Library's comment composer (`ui-library/registry/components/comment-thread.tsx`): the placeholder "Leave a comment… Type @ to mention someone" (写评论… 输入 @ 提及同事; a reply's "Write a reply…"), a Comment or Reply button led by a send icon, and Enter to send, Shift+Enter for a new line (`sendsOnEnter` in `client/pages/crm-detail.tsx`; ⌘/Ctrl+Enter still sends; never while an input method composes, and the mention list, while open, takes Enter to pick a name).
