# Inbox, overview and reports

Read this before changing the inbox (its AI suggestions and notifications), the overview, the reports page, or the menu's counts.

The detail pane is the suggestion's record as its page's left column (`RecordPane` in `client/pages/crm-detail.tsx`, loaded lazily: the heading, the AI chip and actions, the intro and the tabs, without the particulars or the More menu, which `DetailColumns` leaves out under the drawer context's `bare`), opened on its timeline at the AI's card (the context's `focus`, which `HistoryFeed` takes where no address names one), where the card is handled as on the record's page; a white bar above it, over a rule as the record drawer's and as tall as the list's tabs so the two rules meet in one line, staying at the top while the pane scrolls, names the record's kind at its left and opens the full page there at its right, the button's words ending where the content below does, and the record's links open the inbox's drawer; each suggestion's card in the list reads in two lines, its kind as its menu icon (`ENTITY_ICONS` in `client/components/crm/entity-icons.ts`, named on hover and to screen readers), its record and its time, then where it stands, with no AI icon; the list and the tabs above it share their sides (6 units), the cards' words and the group headings starting under the tabs' first icon, each card pulled out by its padding and border so the bar its edge carries stands in the gutter (the notifications' cards alike), and the tabs are as tall as the record drawer's bar. The block carries application changes for this, kept across upgrades: the two views are labelled AI suggestions (To do) and Notifications (All) in its locales, To do comes first and is the default, offered only while a category is offered there (`viewsOf` in `model.ts`; with one view no tabs show), the type filter shows only where a view offers more than one kind (`inbox-list.tsx`), the two views are drawn as a record page's tabs (from the left, each led by its icon, the open one over a 2px line, on a thin rule; the AI suggestions' count a small tag in the AI's tint, the notifications' muted figures; `inbox-list.tsx`), Mark all as read heads the Notifications tab's list, on its first group heading's line, at the left where a view with a single group shows no heading of it (`head`, a slot `inbox-list.tsx` gained), the page draws its heading, Inbox, without the sentence under it (`inbox-page.tsx`, `client/pages/inbox.tsx`), the CRM's notifications are described by the CRM: the page passes the inbox an `InboxSource` (`crmInboxSource` in `client/components/crm/inbox-notices.ts`) that asks `GET /api/notices?ids=` (`noticesOf` in `server/providers/crm/notices.ts`, from the outbox row each notification was sent from: its event, record, who caused it, `crmOutbox.actorId` from `202610100008_crm_outbox_actor`, and when, and for a mention or a reply the comment, the reader's alone) and a renderer for source `crm` knows the record each is about, picking one of the CRM's notifications shows that record in the detail pane as an AI suggestion's pane does (`CrmNoticeDetail` in `inbox-notice-detail.tsx` on the shared `RecordPaneFrame` of `inbox-decisions-view.tsx`, through `Placeholder`, a renderer part `registry.ts` gained, drawn in place of an item's detail and given the way back), its timeline brought to the comment or the reply itself, lit up, the thread's folded replies unfolded around it (a `?focus=` or pane focus naming a reply is found in its comment's thread, `reveal` on `CommentThread`), or on a phone the record's page (`selectParams`, a prop `inbox-page.tsx` gained, which `client/pages/inbox.tsx` answers); a notification the outbox no longer holds (sent before its id was recorded, or its row removed with a demo dataset) is known by its in-app item's route target, with nothing more said of it, and any other item's detail reads its sentence after its title, muted, the sentence carrying the link, the inbox's list sits on the card's white (`inbox-page.tsx`), its cards marking the one selected on the menu's own grey (`bg-sidebar`, a hovered one `--hover`), without a border, a notification's card is laid out as an AI suggestion's: no icon, the bar at its edge marking it unread, its title (which says what happened, so no kind tag) and its time on the first line, its sentence muted on the second, with no chevron and no column kept for its menu, which takes the time's place while the card is hovered, its read title in their weight and its time as compact (`relativeTime` in `model.ts`) (`inbox-item.tsx`), where the AI suggestions' tab has its mine/team switch, rather than sitting in the page's header (the list's `head`, a slot `inbox-list.tsx` gained for it), and the To do count includes a collection's waiting records (`InboxCollectionState.waiting` in `model.ts`, added to `pendingTotal` in `inbox-page.tsx`, since the library counts only entry categories and feeds). No page heading carries a line under it on the overview and the list pages, and the inbox has no heading at all (leads, opportunities, companies, contacts, approvals, tasks), whose `dashboardDescription` and `descriptions` keys are gone. The overview sits in a narrower centred column than the lists and boards (`CrmFrame`'s `narrow`, `max-w-5xl` instead of 1600px). Its heading greets the user by the time of day in the CRM's time zone and by name (`greeting.*`, led by the time of day's icon in the heading's own colour, a sunrise, the sun or the moon, `DAY_PART_ICONS`; the reasons deals were lost in the last 90 days and the paused ones wait; the sales cycle (creation to won over the last year) and each open stage's average stay from the history's `stage` entries, with the open deals waiting longer than one and a half times it (at least 14 days); the lead sources as a section of their own, of the reader's own leads in their own scope; and, for the team, each seller's completed tasks, comments and mail conversations over the last four weeks and this one; it lists the verdicts waiting on the reader, their own leads or, for whoever may edit every lead, the team's (`GET /api/decisions`, `pendingDecisions`; with `state=all` the page also lists every decided one after those waiting (a lead followed up again or converted keeps them; a decided one needs only read access to its lead, a waiting one edit access), while the count, `pending`, is only of those waiting, and the page draws every card whole, as the lead's timeline does), decided in place through the same `VerdictPanel` as the lead page's card, whose "Discuss" (讨论) opens a new conversation in the assistant panel with the lead and the card pinned as a `crm-evaluation` context item, nothing sent (`DiscussButton`, `useDiscuss`), for questions that change nothing, while the box under a waiting card redoes it in the person's words (`POST /api/leads/:id/evaluate` with `revise` and `instruction`, `LeadEvaluator.request`'s `revise`: the words are the person's `aiRevise` feed entry, the new run gets the card and the words and carries the old card's unwritten findings, its entry keeps `revises`, `instruction` and `instructedById`, and the old card is superseded; the field box's Write writes them alone, deciding nothing); a waiting card to follow up or nurture lays out the task its accept creates (`PlannedTask`: type, name, start, owner and description, changeable, the owner only by whoever may assign; the description is built by `planDescription` in `server/providers/crm/plan.ts`, which both sides share, and the list above stops repeating what it holds) and sends what was changed as `task`, applied over the verdict's next step or review; the model reads the lead's tasks by reference (`A1`…, with `overdue`) under `TASK_RULES` (a reference left in text for a person is replaced by the task's name, `unref`), may name the next step's owner from the active salespeople (`ownerChoices`, `nextStep.owner` → `ownerId`, used by `applyStep` unless the person changes it on the card) when the lead or the person's instruction asks for someone, and a next step naming one (`nextStep.taskId`) carries on that planned task instead of planning another: accepting updates it (each part the step brings replacing the one under the same heading in its description, `mergePlanDescription` in `server/providers/crm/plan.ts`, what a person wrote kept; the card says what that changes through `planPartChanges`, or that nothing does; its time kept unless changed), records its outcome when the person says it was done, overdue or not (`mode: 'report'`;
