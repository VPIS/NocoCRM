# AI capture and duplicate leads

Read this before changing New lead or New opportunity with the AI, the capture review, or finding and merging duplicate leads.

New lead opens with the AI first while the assistant is enabled (`client/components/crm/lead-capture.tsx`; the form directly otherwise; `CaptureTabs` atop both dialogs switches between them, the list page keeping the pasted text until either is closed): pasted text goes to `POST /api/leads/capture` (`server/providers/crm/lead-capture.ts`), which asks the CRM's model through the Agents plugin's model gateway (`openSession` and `askJson` in `server/providers/crm/ai-model.ts`), the records it checks read as the signed-in user. It saves nothing: it returns prefilled leads, the fields the model was unsure of in `uncertain` and possible duplicates looked up through `CrmService.list`. One review shows them, however many: a list (with several) of each lead's state and owner beside the lead form itself (`RecordForm`'s `embedded` mode, fields only, values reported back), required fields left empty highlighted, owner and source settable for all (filling only what neither the AI nor the user set on a lead), and, in a panel on the right that stays in view beside the form, the pasted text, editable there and organised again on request (which replaces the leads not yet created), above an instruction box that sends the leads as they stand with the user's words to the same endpoint, which returns them revised under their keys (`ref`), every AI field included and null where cleared, plus a `reply`; the AI may name an owner only for a user who may assign, from the active salespeople. Creating takes the checked leads that are complete and leaves the rest.

The capture writes every value in the pasted text's own language, places included: the field descriptions in `server/providers/crm/lead-capture.ts` ask for the province and the city as the text writes them, never translated or romanised (an English example there, Zhejiang Province, had the model write a Chinese text's city as Hangzhou); the province is then turned into its code (`subdivisionCode`, which knows the Chinese names), the city kept as written. Its dialog's button and tab read AI 整理 (`leadCapture.organise`, `leadCapture.aiTab`).

The old "Capture lead" shortcuts, which sent a prompt to the chat, are gone, and so are the lead page's follow-up, research and conversion shortcuts, which the follow-up covers. The pasted text is not kept with the lead, as in NocoProject's intake: only the fields the user confirms are saved, and the run creates no conversation that would keep the text.

New opportunity opens with the AI too while the assistant is on (`client/components/crm/opportunity-capture.tsx`, `OpportunityCaptureDialog`, the same `CaptureTabs` beside the form): `POST /api/opportunities/capture` (`server/providers/crm/opportunity-capture.ts`, `captureOpportunity`, prompt in English, titles in `server/locales/crm-ai/deal-capture.ts`) returns one prefilled opportunity, its company matched by `findDuplicates` on the company the text names rather than by the model, the people it names matched to that company's contacts (`matchCompany`, again through `POST /opportunities/matchCompany` when the person picks another company, which the embedded form now allows) and the company's open deals as duplicates; the people not matched stay in the form's contacts field as rows carrying `person` instead of a `contactId` (`contactRows`; the field draws them as `NewContact`, marked new) and are created as the company's contacts with the opportunity unless their row is removed; an adjustment sends the field's rows, which the server names (`peopleOf`, a contact by its record) so the model keeps, changes or drops each person. A contact picker in that field does not offer a contact already in another row (`RecordPicker`'s `exclude`). Creating posts `opportunities?via=capture`, recorded as organised by the AI, and the create route follows a new opportunity up (`dealEvaluator().request`) unless `followUp=false`, which the form's and the dialog's "AI follows up the opportunity at once" send when unticked.

Duplicate leads are found and merged in `server/providers/crm/lead-merge.ts` (`GET /api/leads/:id/duplicates`, `POST /leads/:id/proposeMerge`, `POST /leads/:id/merge` with `:id` the lead kept) and `client/components/crm/lead-merge.tsx` (`LeadDuplicates` atop a lead's particulars, a card with the groups' border and shadow on a faint wash of the warning colour, each duplicate one sentence that wraps where its line ends ("Same email as …", 与 … 邮箱相同), ending in Merge, its owner and date on hover, its merge dialog on the registry `RadioGroup`): open leads only, matched by plain rules (the same email, a phone ending the other's, the same person at a company named alike), never by the model; the AI only proposes which lead to keep and which values are right, the plain proposal standing in when it is off or fails. A merge asks for edit access to both leads, not the right to delete, because nothing is lost: the other lead's values stay on the kept lead's `merged` history entry (which `historyOf` passes through by the lead's readable fields), its tasks and comments move, its waiting follow-ups are superseded, and it is deleted directly rather than through `remove()`; the kept lead is followed up anew (trigger `merged`) unless unticked. The AI capture review checks for duplicates through the same rules (`findDuplicates`, which also lists, without offering a merge, leads of the same company, contacts by email or phone and companies by name) and offers "Merge into it…" beside a lead of the same person: nothing is created; the same `MergeDialog` takes the reviewed values as `input` (the other side, id `new`, `UNSAVED`), the held lead is always kept, only the fields the new values fill are asked about, and the merge saves the picks into the held lead as organised by the AI (`via: 'capture'`) with a `merged` entry marked `captured`.
