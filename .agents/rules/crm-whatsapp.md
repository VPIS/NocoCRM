# WhatsApp

Read this before changing the company's WhatsApp number in the CRM: its webhook, the conversations, a record's WhatsApp tab and timeline lines, what the follow-ups read from WhatsApp, or its connection page.

Phase 1 receives only: messages from customers to the company's number come in through Meta's Cloud API and are read in the CRM; replies are made on WhatsApp (sending, templates, consent and usage are phase 2). The design is NocoSolution's `NocoCRM/WhatsApp 接入方案.md`.

**Webhook.** `server/routes/whatsapp.ts` contributes a root route (`defineRootRoutes`, so `/<base path>/webhooks/whatsapp`, outside `/api`): GET answers Meta's subscription check with `hub.challenge` only for the verify token the CRM made; POST is public because Meta presents no session, and keeps a delivery only when `X-Hub-Signature-256` is the app secret's HMAC of the raw body (`verifySignature` in `server/providers/crm/whatsapp/channel.ts`), storing it as it came in `crmWaInbox` and answering 200 at once; unsigned or wrongly signed posts get 401. Meta redelivers for up to seven days and in no order: the pass processes deliveries in arrival order and messages by Meta's timestamp, and drops a message whose `waMessageId` is stored (unique index). A delivery that fails five times is `failed` with a code (`unknownNumber`, `invalidJson`, `processingFailed`; anything unexpected is logged, never stored), shown on the connection page and retried from there.

**The channel.** `channel.ts` is Meta's Cloud API alone (Graph `v25.0`): `webhookEvents` reads a delivery into messages and statuses (business-scoped `user_id`/`from_user_id`, the number when given, username and profile name, media ids and the five-minute `url`, a `user_changed_number` system message), `cloudApiChannel` downloads media (checking `sha256`, refusing over 100 MB) and reads a number's standing. Everything else depends only on its types, so a provider proxying the Cloud API replaces the base address, the authorization and the signature check there. Tests fake `fetch`.

**The service.** `WhatsAppService` (`server/providers/crm/whatsapp/service.ts`, `whatsappToken`, registered and wired by `CrmProvider`) keeps the connection (one number in `crmWaNumbers`; the access token sealed in its row and the app secret and verify token in the `crmSettings` row `whatsapp`, by the secrets service, purpose `crm.whatsapp`; saving needs `SECRETS_KEYS`, else `secretsMissing` 503), and runs a pass every 10 s on `CrmProvider`'s timers (`process()`: deliveries, then media, then follow-ups). A customer (`crmWaContacts`) is found by their user id (`crmWaIdentities`, every id they have had; a changed number adds the new id and marks the old replaced), else their number (`waId`), else created; a new one is linked when exactly one unconverted lead or contact has their number (`samePhone` from `lead-merge.ts`), else stays `unlinked`. States: `unlinked`, `linked`, `dismissed` (not a customer), `spam`. A conversation is a number × customer (`crmWaConversations`: handler, last inbound time for the 24-hour window, unread count, `followUpDueAt`); messages are `crmWaMessages` (direction, origin `customer`/`crm`, and `businessApp`/`imported` reserved for coexistence; media id, status `pending`/`stored`/`unavailable` and the CRM file it was stored as).

**Records and timeline.** A linked customer reaches the lead, or the contact with the contact's company and the deals naming the contact (`targetsOf`). Each reached record gets one `crmHistory` row a customer and business day (`action` and `source` `whatsapp`, `sourceKey` `<customer id>:<YYYY-MM-DD>`, actor `system`, `changes` with the counts in and out, who wrote last and the customer's name), rewritten from that day's messages, so redelivery and relinking agree (`writeTimeline`, `rewriteTimeline` after a link change). `historyOf` passes its `changes` through; the client draws it as `WhatsAppEvent` in `client/pages/crm-detail.tsx`, the customer's name in the actor slot, opening the WhatsApp tab. `lastExchanges` counts `whatsapp` rows as contact (`exchanges.ts`), and a lead with one turns working (`leadWorked`). Converting a lead moves its customers to the new contact, merging moves them to the lead kept, deleting a lead or contact unlinks them (`CrmService.whatsappRecords`, called inside those transactions). The media files are kept by the file sweep while a message names them (`file-sweep.ts`), and read through the attachments' content route, which asks the conversation (`CrmService.whatsappFileReadable`).

**Who reads what.** A conversation linked to a record is read by whoever may read that record (`mayRead`); one linked to none by whoever has the page grant `crm-whatsapp` (both sales sets carry it; existing sets received it once, marker `crm-whatsapp-v1`). The conversation endpoints also check that grant; changing a linked customer's link or state needs edit access to the record they are linked to, linking needs edit access to the new one. The connection page is the CRM settings' (`manage`), and so are its endpoints.

**Notices and follow-ups.** The first unread message of a conversation queues a notice to its handler (the linked record's owner, else the number's receptionist, `defaultHandlerId`) under the record-changes switch: on the record (event `whatsapp`, opening its WhatsApp tab), or, for a customer linked to none, on the conversation itself (`crmOutbox.entity` `whatsapp`, event `whatsappNew`, opening `/crm/whatsapp/<id>`; `deliverNotifications` has a branch for it). A customer's recent message on a linked conversation sets `followUpDueAt` ten minutes on (`QUIET_MS`); once it passes quietly, the lead and the customer's open deals are followed up through the same `customerReplied` as a mail reply (one switch for both). The lead and deal follow-ups and task preparation read the record's WhatsApp messages (`whatsappOf`, the last 180 days, at most 30, as the customer's words under "# WhatsApp with …", `whatsappSection`).

**Pages.** The menu's Integrations section has WhatsApp after Mailbox (`crm-whatsapp`, `/crm/whatsapp`, WhatsApp's own mark as its icon, `WhatsAppLogo` in `client/components/crm/whatsapp/logo.tsx`, since lucide has no brand icons, its count the unread messages the viewer answers, `useWhatsAppUnreadCount`): `client/pages/crm-whatsapp/` — the list (`?view=mine|unlinked|all`, polled every 15 s) beside the open conversation (`/crm/whatsapp/:conversationId`, polled every 10 s), its link dialog (`…/link`) and new-lead form (`…/new-lead`, the CRM's `RecordForm`); the page hosts the record drawer (`?open=`). The messages are `WhatsAppMessages` (`client/components/crm/whatsapp/messages.tsx`: day separators, bubbles, media, Load earlier, a New messages button, pinned to the newest while the reader is there), the record tab is `RecordWhatsAppPanel` (`record-tab.tsx`, on leads, contacts, companies and deals while `whatsapp.configured` is in the workspace metadata, which is true while a number is connected or the demo dataset's number is there, polled every 30 s), failed loads are `WhatsAppFailure` (`failure.tsx`). The connection is `client/pages/crm-whatsapp/connection.tsx` at `/crm/whatsapp/connection` (route `crm-whatsapp-connection`, no menu entry), reached from the WhatsApp page's header "Manage WhatsApp connection" (管理 WhatsApp 连接) for whoever manages the CRM's settings, as the mail accounts are from the mailbox's (it was a Settings tab until 2026-10-15): the connection, the webhook address and verify token, and the status with Check connection and Retry failed deliveries. Copy is under `whatsapp`, `whatsappSettings`, `feed.whatsapp` in `client/locales/crm/index.ts`; notice titles in `server/locales/crm-notifications`.

**The demo number.** The demo dataset writes a number of mode `demo` with seven conversations ([crm-demo.md](crm-demo.md)). It receives nothing and is never the connection: `number()`, and the receptionist a relinked customer falls back to, leave it out.
