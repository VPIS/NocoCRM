# The AI assistant and the Agents plugin

Read this before changing the assistant people talk to (Sales Expert), its dock, ask boxes, panel and conversations page, the business guidance, the conversation rules, plans, or how agents call the CRM.

The assistant people talk to is an online agent of the Agents plugin named Sales Expert, in a panel of the CRM's own (both described with the Agents plugin below). Its dock can be switched off for the whole company on Settings → General (the setting `assistantDock`, the Business defaults card's switch, on by default; the workspace metadata carries it to every page): the header's ✨ button stays. It is reached from the header's ✨ button (`AssistantHeaderButton` in `client/components/crm/assistant-header-button.tsx`, first of the header's actions, an application change to `client/layouts/components/header-actions.tsx`, since 2026-10-16), on every page, the ones without the dock included: it toggles the panel as the plugin's ⌘J does (`toggleChat`), opening it where the person left it, the last conversation, and closing it again, a new conversation being the panel's +; disabled with the reason while the assistant cannot answer; and from the dock (`AssistantDock` in `client/components/crm/assistant-dock.tsx`, mounted in the application layout's `<main>`), the record pages' "Ask AI" having been removed: a box floating over the bottom of the main column (`z-20`, centred, 22rem wide closed and 32rem open; on a phone, below the 768px of `useIsMobile`, a round button in the corner that opens a new conversation with the page's record pinned and nothing sent, `useDiscuss`), hidden while the panel is open, on the overview (`/`, which opens on an ask box of its own in the page flow, `AssistantAskBox` in `client/components/crm/assistant-ask-box.tsx`, with, under it, at most three questions its attention figures raise (`ASK_FROM` in `client/pages/crm-dashboard.tsx`, the most pressing first, each only while its figure is above nought, its count in its words, `overviewAsk.*`, sent as it reads), asking for the judgement the cards below do not give and nothing the inbox already holds (its suggestions, approvals and notices); the same three for everyone it had went on 2026-10-16, and the sales analysis they once ended with is the reports page's header button; composed like the chat's own composer: the question grows past one line (Enter sends, Shift+Enter breaks the line), files are attached with its paperclip, dropped or pasted, and uploaded at once through the plugin's uploads (`useAskFiles`), and the model the new conversation runs on is picked beside them (`useAgentModels`, the agent's model entries, remembered in the browser); both boxes share the draft and the return of focus in `client/components/crm/assistant-ask.ts`, and both wear the AI border, `.crm-ai-border` in `client/styles.css`: the primary colour alone, mixed far toward the card's white so the line reads as faint as the other borders, with a faint AI glow, a thin halo and a short shadow, both firmer while the box has focus), on `/crm/mail`, `/inbox` and `/crm/whatsapp` with its conversations and connection page (filled by fixed panes it would cover) and on the agents' conversations page (`/crm/agent-chat`, which has a composer of its own), outside the application layout (settings included) and while the agents load, and disabled with the reason while the assistant cannot answer (`useAgentReadiness` in `client/components/crm/agent-chat/ask.ts`; `usePanelOpen` in `assistant-ask.ts` is the panel's open state). One line until it is clicked, it then opens, a little wider, into the composer the overview's box uses (`AskComposer` in `client/components/crm/assistant-ask-composer.tsx`, the draft in `useAskDraft`): the question growing past one line, the paperclip and the model; it closes to one line once focus leaves it empty, or on a press outside it (a page that drags on a press, the board, keeps focus where it was); while focused it lays a veil over the whole page under it, drawn as a dialog's backdrop (`data-dock-veil`: the backdrop's tint and blur, over the header's z-40, the box raised to z-40 above it while in use, every overlay at z-50 still above both), which a click beside the box lands on, closing it, so the click never reaches the list or the board behind; staying open while its model menu is open or it holds text or files, and not on a switch to another window such as the file picker. Focused, it lists the page's suggested questions above it (by the record's kind on a record page, else by path, keys `dock.q.*` in the `crm` locale); a typed question (Enter, never during an IME composition) or a suggestion starts a new conversation through `useAskAgent`, with the page's record pinned when there is one, and opens the panel on it, focus coming back to the dock when the panel closes. Record pages announce their record (`useAnnounceRecord` in `client/components/crm/current-record.ts`, from `LeadDetail` and `EntityDetail`, not from the record drawer, whose backdrop covers the dock; each page withdraws only its own announcement), and the dock's placeholder names its kind ("Ask anything about this opportunity…", `dock.placeholderFor.<entity>`; elsewhere "Ask anything…"). Every page keeps 6rem empty at the bottom of what scrolls under the dock (`CrmFrame`'s `pb-24`, the record page's right column, the board's columns), and a record page's left column, whose timeline ends under it, enough for the box open as well (`pb-36` on its content, which never shrinks to the column; the WhatsApp tab, whose messages scroll inside its card, `pb-20`, the closed box alone, `has-[[data-slot=record-whatsapp]]`), so its last row scrolls above it. The user's conversations with the assistant are the full page `/crm/agent-chat` (对话), which has no menu entry (its route in `client/routes.ts` declares only a `breadcrumb`): the overview lists the latest four under its ask box (`RecentConversations` in `client/components/crm/agent-chat/recent-conversations.tsx`, each opening in the panel, an unread one in bold with the AI's dot) with View all (查看全部) leading there; the list the sidebar once kept under the menu (`SidebarChats`) is gone. The assistant reads the CRM through the CRM's own routes run as commands, with exactly the access of the person who woke it, and every change it asks for is a plan the person decides (both under the Agents plugin below). The deployment's business guidance is shipped in `server/providers/crm/guidance.ts` (`crmGuidance(days, language)`, NocoBase's own sales rules and published editions, which the demo dataset follows) and may be written over by an administrator on the Business guidance page in the Agent team section (`client/pages/crm-guidance.tsx` at `/crm/guidance`, checking the settings' `settings/crm manage`, the setting `businessGuidance` saved on its own, Markdown up to a long text's length, blank for the shipped one; the page shows the shipped one, in the AI language with the reminders' days, until then, and Restore the default clears it). `settingsGuidance(settings)` in the same file, which the settings page imports too, gives the one in force; it is part of the rules every Agents conversation on the CRM gets (`server/providers/crm-agents/rules.ts`) and the system prompt of every background run (`ai-model.ts`). The former AI business instructions setting is gone. The approval standards (what discount, payment terms, contract terms and delivery commitments need an approval) are written only in the `opportunity-follow-up` and `approval-review` skills, identically (`tests/logic/approval-standards.test.ts` checks it); the guidance and the follow-up prompt refer to them without numbers. The CRM's former chat service, its `/api/crm/ai/*` routes, the action centre page and the `crmAiMessages` / `crmAiActions` tables are gone (`202609220001_crm_drop_ai_tables`), and so is the AI Employee plugin the assistant moved to after them, with its Registry UI (`client/extensions/nocobase-ai`), the CRM employee and its read tools and `ASK` write tools with their confirmation cards (`server/ai/`), and `POST /api/screenProposal`. The CRM's own AI switch is gone too: the AI is on whenever there is a model to ask, which the metadata reports as `assistant` (`CrmService.assistantEnabled` asks `aiCheck`, which `CrmProvider` sets to `aiAvailable` in `server/providers/crm/ai-model.ts`) and which the AI's background work, the captures, the inbox's AI suggestions, the reports page's analysis and the global search's Ask AI follow, while the dock, the overview's box, the sidebar's conversations and the cards' Discuss follow the agent's own readiness; a stored `aiEnabled` setting is dropped on read and on save.

An agent reads the overview and the reports in one call, `crm dashboard` (`GET /api/dashboard` declared as a command, asking the search's action since it reads only what the reader may read; `--scope team` for the team's), and the conversation rules (`rules.ts`) tell it so, list the commands and the shape of their output (`{ ok, result: { data, meta } }`, a get's `.result.data.record`), point it from the attention counts to their records (`statistics`' `members`, then `list --ids`), keep it from the method skills on questions across the pipeline, and have it run independent commands in one shell call and never cut output with `head -c`, which in the plugin's shell splits Chinese into unreadable bytes: a run that first explored the help and the JSON took a minute where these take half that, nearly all of it waiting for the model between steps. Counts, totals and trends come from each object's summary route (`GET /api/<entity>/summary`, the agents' command `<entity> summary`; `CrmService.summarize`, which reads the rows through the list's own scope and filters, `filtered`, and groups them in `server/providers/crm/summary.ts` by a field, a participant or a period of the business time zone, with sums per currency). Text a model writes never makes the browser fetch another origin: the CRM's `Markdown`, which also draws the assistant's messages, renders images through `client/components/safe-image.tsx`, which loads only this origin's images and shows any other as a link, and `index.html` sets the page policy `img-src 'self' data: blob:`; this closes the image-URL exfiltration that published prompt-injection attacks on AI CRMs used. Neither the assistant nor the background runs search the web (the conversation rules tell the assistant to say so when a question needs it). What a conversation concludes reaches the CRM only through plans, and the conversation rules (`rules.ts`) say how: something that took place with a customer (a call, a meeting, a visit) is a comment on the record it concerns or the outcome of the open task that planned it, proposed in a plan like any change; mail is never recorded (it reaches the timeline by itself); a lead's original message is never changed; and an AI card in the page context is read from its record's history before it is discussed, never decided for the person, a different course being proposed as a plan.

The CRM setting `aiLanguage` (labelled AI working language: `en-US` by default or `zh-CN`; a stored `user` from before reads as the default) is what the AI writes in when it works on its own (follow-ups, approval reviews, the capture's replies, merge proposals, the daily checks' task titles), the same for the whole team, and the language of the business guidance the assistant's conversations are given; the assistant answers in the language the person writes in (the CRM assistant preset's instructions). The business guidance has both versions in `server/providers/crm/guidance.ts` (`crmGuidance(days, language)`), while the CRM's four skills (`ai/skills/*/SKILL.md`) are written in English alone, whatever the AI language: the model follows English instructions and writes in the language it is told. Their frontmatter keeps their title and description as display keys (`i18n: { namespace: crm }`, with entries in `client/locales/`), as the AI Employee plugin's skill pages read them; the Agents plugin's library holds the whole file with its English description (`provisionSkills`), and the body stays English; `tests/logic/approval-standards.test.ts` checks those locale entries, that each frontmatter parses, and that the two skills' approval standards agree. The conversation rules read the settings `CrmAgentsProvider` holds, refreshed whenever the settings are saved (`CrmService.settingsSaved`), since the plugin calls them where nothing may be read.

The Agents plugin (`@nocobase/app-plugin-agents`, registered in all three roots, configured by `server/config/agents.ts`: its CLI is named `crm` and the application `nococrm`) carries the whole of the CRM's AI (it gave agents the CRM's commands and plans, made the assistant people talk to an agent of it, and moved the background work onto its model gateway, `server/providers/crm/ai-model.ts`, replacing the AI Employee plugin). Its online agents run the CRM's own routes as commands: a route declares its command with `runCommand(action, options)` in `server/routes/context.ts` (the plugin's `cliRoute` plus the run token in its security), named explicitly (`leads list`, `leads convert`, `plan create`…), and `crmGuard` takes keys scoped to a run (`required({ scopedKeys: true })`), refusing an action the run does not hold with `ACTION_NOT_ALLOWED` and every non-reading action but proposing a plan with `PLAN_REQUIRED` (`metadata.reason: 'alwaysConfirm'`). What a run holds is `crmActionGate` in `server/providers/crm-agents/gate.ts`: the agent's actions, within what the person who woke it holds (`heldActions`, from the CRM's own grants under the run's key scope), within what may be granted at all (`agentActionOptions` in `actions.ts`: deleting, deciding approvals and exporting never). `CrmAgentsProvider` (`server/providers/crm-agents/`) registers the actions, the gate, the skill `crm-plans` (`skill.ts`: the commands, the plan's JSON and its rules), the preset "CRM assistant" (`preset.ts`), the conversation source `crm`, and creates the plugin's model services from `ai.llmServices` when missing, named by their key and titled as config.yml writes them (`model-services.ts`, `@temporary(nocobase-official)` until the plugin reads them itself). Every change an agent asks for is a plan (`server/providers/crm/plans/engine.ts`, `CrmPlans`, `@temporary(nocobase-official)`, modelled on the projects plugin's plans): `POST /api/plans` rehearses every step as the person inside a transaction rolled back (a step refused names itself, `planStepRefused`, field `steps.N`) and keeps it for them alone (`crmPlans`, `crmPlanRows`, migration `202610100006_crm_plans`); `POST /api/plans/:planId/execute` (with the revision and the steps to skip) carries it out in one transaction as the person, `stale` when a record it changes was updated since it was rehearsed, and `void` declines it; `PATCH /api/plans/:planId/steps/:position` (`CrmPlans.revise`, the person's alone, no run token) lays new values over those of a waiting step that creates or updates a record, rehearses the whole plan again (a value refused names the step, `PLAN_STEP_REFUSED`, and changes nothing) and moves its revision on, so the card edits each such value as the record pages do (`StepValue` in `plan-card.tsx`, a value naming a record an earlier step creates staying as it reads); a plan expires after a day; every history entry it writes carries `origin` (the plan, the agent, the run) on `crmHistory`; and the outcome goes back into the agent's conversation as news (`plan-news.ts`), waking the agent. The people decide plans on a card, a plan being called suggested changes (建议的改动) wherever the interface names it, since people do not know the word (`PlanCard` in `client/components/crm/plan-card.tsx`, data in `plans.ts`, drawn as the AI's cards in a timeline are: on the card's white, waiting or decided, each step a white card of its own with its fields in a label column, the decision under the steps with "Carry out" in the primary colour): each step's record and changes, an update's old value struck through beside the new one, a checkbox to leave a step out, "Carry out N changes" and "Ignore" (忽略), each with its icon as the AI cards' buttons, a plan being carried out, decided, stale, failed, expired or gone told on its line, focus moving to that line once decided. The card appears in its conversation and, on the record pages it would change, as "Waiting on you" atop the particulars (`WaitingPlans`, drawn by `DetailColumns` from its `recordId`); it is not in the inbox (no category, no count in its badge), the agent's message that comes with it making its conversation unread, which the overview's latest conversations mark. The conversations with the agents have a full page, `/crm/agent-chat` (`client/pages/crm-agent-chat/`, laid out as the inbox is: the heading All conversations (全部对话, which the breadcrumb shows too) over a rule, Collapse (收起) beside New conversation at its right, returning to the page the reader came from past the conversations opened since (the router's history `idx` when the page opened; opened first, the overview) with the conversation open, if any, in the side panel, the panel's Open full page undone (it replaced a Back on a row of its own) with no line under it, the list in a 24rem column on the card's white (the inbox's list as wide and as white, `inbox-page.tsx`), the conversation beside it, whose scroller reaches into the side padding around it so its scrollbar stands clear of the messages; `/new` to start one with a picked agent, archived ones under `?archived=1`, a plan step's record opening in the record drawer), the assistant panel's full-page view, built like the panel on the plugin's headless chat (`@nocobase/app-plugin-agents/client/chat`) because the UI Library's `agent-chat` block needs a package not on npm; the route's page grant is `crm-agent-chat` (the sales sets received it once, marker `crm-agent-chat-v1`), it has no menu entry, the overview's View all leading to it, the assistant dock hides there, and a plan card's "from a conversation" opens its conversation in the panel. The plugin's own pages are routed in the menu's Agent team section, after the CRM's four agents (`crm-agent-group` in `client/routes.ts`: Agents, Skills, Models, Usage, each shown only to whoever its own grant lets open it). They are drawn in the CRM's look without changing the plugin, whose pages carry their own copies of the registry primitives: `crmStyled` in `client/routes.ts` wraps each one's loaded component in `withCrmStyle` (`client/components/crm/plugin-page.tsx`, a `display: contents` element of class `crm-plugin-page`), and the `.crm-plugin-page` rules in `client/styles.css` give its bordered boxes and cards the CRM's white, faint border and short shadow, its tables the list pages' header band and cell padding, its line tabs a record page's rule, and the usage chart the primary colour; a plugin update that renames those `data-slot`s or classes loses only the look. An online run may call the model 40 times before it gives up with `stepLimit` (`server.maxSteps` in `server/config/agents.ts`, the plugin's default 16), since a question across the pipeline, the reports page's analysis among them, reads statistics, lists and several deals' histories before answering. The plugin keeps its model keys and runners' secrets under the application's master keys, so `SECRETS_KEYS` must be set (`server/config/secrets.ts`); setting it the first time signs everyone out once. A queued online run that the plugin (0.1.0-beta.3) resumes while the server boots fails, because the API document it reads is not ready before the routes are registered; a run queued while the server is up is unaffected.
