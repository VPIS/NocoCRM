# Company signals

Read this before changing the company signals: what the AI finds on the web that happened to a company someone sells to, its daily check, the Signals tab, or the web search behind it.

A signal is a change outside the CRM that a salesperson would act on, of five kinds (`SIGNAL_TYPES` in `server/providers/crm/signal-reading.ts`): `funding`, `hiring`, `leadership`, `business` (a major launch, an acquisition, a large contract, a listing) and `risk` (layoffs, a lawsuit, trouble). It belongs to its company (`crmSignals`, migration `202610140002_crm_company_signals`), changes none of the company's fields and starts nothing on its own: in the guideline's terms the AI is the company's collaborator, handing what it finds to the owner. Its status is `new` until someone who may edit the company marks it `seen` or `dismissed`; a dismissed event keeps its `eventKey` (its kind, month and wording, `eventKey()`), so it is never proposed again, and the events already known are named to the search so it does not repeat them. Deleting a company deletes its signals (`CrmService.remove`), as removing the demo data does for the demo's companies.

`server/providers/crm/company-signals.ts` (`CompanySignals`, `companySignalsToken`, registered by `CrmProvider`) does the work. The daily check, the scheduler task `crm.company-signals` at 09:30 (texts in `server/locales/crm-ai/schedules.ts`, switched off on the scheduled tasks page), looks at the companies with an open deal and those someone switched `crmCompanies.signalsWatched` on, each at most once every `CHECK_EVERY_DAYS` (7) after `signalsCheckedAt`, the longest unchecked first, at most 20 a pass, one at a time; a company whose check failed keeps its old `signalsCheckedAt`, so the next pass tries it first. Someone who may edit a company may check it at once (`POST /api/companies/:companyId/signals/check`), whether or not it is watched. A check asks one question with the web search on (`signalQuestion`: the company's name, website, industry and city, the last `SIGNAL_WINDOW_DAYS` (30), the kinds, routine news left out, the known events, the AI language) and reads the answer (`readSignals`): an event is kept only when it is of a tracked kind, dated within the window and cites pages the search returned as `[ref_n]`, its sources being those pages, never a URL the model wrote (asked for links, it made up plausible ones). An answer in prose (the model often ignores the JSON instruction while citing) is asked again without searching to put the same events into JSON (`signalJsonQuestion`), its marks still naming the first answer's pages. Each kept signal writes a line on the company's timeline (`crmHistory` action `signal`, source `ai`, actor `system`, its `changes` the kind, title, day and first source, passed through whole by `historyOf` like a comment's) and a notice to the company's owner and its open deals' owners (`CrmService.notifySignal`, event `signal`, under the record-change notifications switch, opening the Signals tab). Both calls are recorded on the Agents plugin's usage page as the Background AI's (`recordUse`).

The web search is `server/providers/crm/web-research.ts`, a temporary implementation (`@temporary(nocobase-official)`): the Agents plugin's model gateway has no web search and its Alibaba provider drops DashScope's `enable_search`, so it calls DashScope's native API (`multimodal-generation`, China region, `search_options` with `enable_source` and citations; the OpenAI-compatible mode returns no pages) with the key of the Qwen service in `ai.llmServices` (`webSearcher`: the first `dashscope` service with a key, its `qwen3.8-flash` first), reasoning off. Without such a service nothing searches: the Signals tab says so, checking now answers `signalsUnavailable`, and the daily pass does nothing. A search takes 10 to 25 seconds and a few thousand input tokens.

The routes are `server/routes/signals.ts` (`GET /companies/:companyId/signals` for whoever reads the company; `PUT …/signals/watch`, `POST …/signals/check` and `PATCH /signals/:signalId` for whoever edits it), guarded with the CRM's paths (`signals` added in `server/routes/crm.ts`). The client is the company page's Signals tab (`client/components/crm/company-signals.tsx`, tab `signals` in `crm-entity-detail.tsx`, no count) and the timeline's `SignalEvent` in `crm-detail.tsx`; strings under `signals` in `client/locales/crm/index.ts`. The demo dataset writes a real, neutral event a search found on one of its project companies (GAC's restructuring announcement of 2026-09-14, with its real sources and date) with its timeline line (`project.signals` in `demo-content/projects-*.ts`), so the demo shows a signal without a model; never an invented event, since the demo's companies are real.

What counts is the method skill `company-signals` (`ai/skills/company-signals/SKILL.md`, in `METHOD_SKILLS`, so it is put into the Agents plugin's skill library once and attached to the assistant, and an administrator edits it on the Skills page; `readSkill` reads the library's version first). It is sent as the search's **system** message (`searchWeb`'s `system`), never in the question: the search is formed from the user message, and with the method in it the search went after the method's own words (searching "signals" found companies named after signals). With it, the model also left out sister companies with similar names, which it otherwise reported (神州信息 for 神州数码). The question keeps the rules the reading enforces whatever the skill says.

Each check is a row of `crmSignalChecks` (migration `202610150001_crm_signal_checks`): the company, its trigger (`daily`, or `asked` with who asked), `found`, `nothing` or `failed` with the error, how many it found and how many deal follow-ups it started; each signal names its check (`checkId`, migration `202610150002_crm_signal_check_ids`; null for the demo's and those from before). Deleting a company, or the demo, deletes its checks. The checks are the agent `signals` of the Auto follow-up page (`SWITCHED_AGENTS` in `agents.ts`, triggers `daily`; it is not in `AGENTS`, whose runs are `ai` entries): its switches live with the others' (the daily pass does nothing while it or its `daily` is off; checking now is never switched off), and `GET /api/aiAgents/signals` answers `CompanySignals.overview` in the agents' overview shape: its runs the checks on companies the reader may read, newest first, a check's summary the titles it found or its error; its stats the checks, the failed ones, and the signals' `seen`, `dismissed` and `new` as accepted, dismissed and pending, the acceptance their seen share. The page (`crm-agents.tsx`) names those figures by `agents.signals.stats`, filters a check by `found`, `nothing` and `failed`, and opens a check's company.

A signal worth acting on (every kind but `hiring`, `ACTING_TYPES`) starts the follow-up of each of its company's open deals (not paused, won or lost) as the deal's owner, through the deal follow-up's trigger `companySignal` (in `AGENT_TRIGGERS.opportunities`, switched on the deal follow-up's tab; `CompanySignals`' `followUp`, wired by `CrmProvider` to `OpportunityEvaluator.request`), unless the deal already has a card waiting or being worked on. The deal follow-up always reads its company's signals of the last 30 days that are not dismissed as its material (`# Company news` in its prompt, `signalsOf` in `opportunity-evaluation.ts`), whatever started it.
